What the report says

CMHC publishes annual rental market reports for major Canadian centres. We use that data to benchmark neighbourhood rent levels.

Neighbourhood averages

Average rents are aggregated at the neighbourhood level, allowing you to see how your rent compares locally.

Market pressure

We highlight the percentage difference between your rent and the average so you can spot overpayment quickly.

Rental type insights

Unit type matters. Compare the same unit size so you get a fair and accurate benchmark.

How we use CMHC data

The CMHC Rental Market Report provides average monthly rents by neighbourhood and unit type. We’ve loaded sample entries from the latest report to power this prototype. Swap in the full CMHC dataset when ready.

Source

Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation (CMHC), Rental Market Report.
